Buying from out of state is hard. Here’s how I make it work.

Most out-of-state buyers share the same fears: overpaying for a home they’ve never walked through, missing something obvious in a video tour, not understanding the local market. Those are legitimate concerns. Here’s how I address all of them.

01

Market briefing before we look at a single house

Before we tour anything, I walk you through exactly what the market looks like in the areas you’re considering — pending ratios, price trends, days on market. You’ll know whether you have negotiating leverage or you’re competing with other buyers.

02

FaceTime tours with nothing held back

I walk every room, open every closet, check the basement, look at the mechanicals, and narrate what I see — including what concerns me. I also drive the neighborhood so you can see the street, the surroundings, and the commute. You ask questions in real time.

03

Local contractor connections before you make an offer

If a home is older or has obvious deferred maintenance, I’ll connect you with a local contractor to get a realistic read on costs before you’re committed. You should know what you’re getting into before you sign anything.

04

You close remotely. No trip required.

Ohio allows remote closings. You don’t have to fly out. We coordinate everything digitally — inspection, appraisal, title, closing. Clients have taken ownership of their new Ohio home without ever booking a flight.

Why Ohio?

For most buyers moving from the coasts, Ohio offers significantly more home for the money. The median Ohio home value is around $255,000. In Central Ohio specifically, you’re getting established neighborhoods, strong school districts, and reasonable property taxes compared to most major metros.

Common questions from out-of-state buyers

Can I buy a home in Ohio without visiting in person?

Yes. I’ve helped buyers from Washington state, California, and across the country purchase Ohio homes entirely remotely. I conduct FaceTime tours, send neighborhood drive videos, and coordinate with local contractors so you have a complete picture before making an offer.

What areas of Ohio do you cover for out-of-state buyers?

I’m based in Central Ohio and primarily cover Columbus, Granville, Newark, and the surrounding counties. If your search takes you elsewhere in Ohio, I can connect you with a trusted agent in that area.

How do FaceTime home tours work?

I walk through every room, open every closet, check the basement, and narrate what I’m seeing — the good and the concerns. I also drive the neighborhood so you can see the street and surrounding area. You ask questions in real time, just like being there.

How do I know if I’m overpaying for an Ohio home?

I track Ohio housing data at the county and ZIP code level — pending ratios, price cuts, days on market. Before you make an offer, I’ll walk you through exactly where that market stands so you know what leverage you have. You can also see the live data here.

Is Ohio housing affordable compared to where I’m moving from?

For most buyers moving from coastal markets, yes — significantly. The median Ohio home value is around $255,000. Central Ohio in particular offers established neighborhoods and reasonable property taxes. See the latest market updates for current data.

How do I get started?

Reach out through the contact form. I’ll schedule a call to understand your situation — budget, timeline, what you’re looking for — and we’ll go from there. No pressure.
